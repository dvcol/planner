# Privacy policy draft

Prepared on 31 October 2026 for the personal, unlisted Planner application.
The final app name, public policy URL and support contact need owner approval.
This file has not been published as a policy.

## Your plans

Planner stores your items, notes, links, locations, labels, lists, itineraries
and schedules locally on your device. It does not operate a developer-hosted
planning service or include advertising or analytics services.

Optional synchronization uses your Apple Account's private iCloud database.
Devices must use the same iCloud account to share plans. Local storage remains
the working copy when private iCloud is unavailable. Planner does not fall back
to a public or shared CloudKit database. Apple operates iCloud under its own
[privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Maps, previews and websites

Showing maps, resolving addresses or Maps links, calculating routes and loading
link previews can contact Apple or the linked provider. Requests may include
the address, coordinates or URL needed for the feature and normal network
information. Temporarily resolved preview information is separate from the
planning content you explicitly save.

Opening a website inside Planner contacts that website. The native web viewer
uses persistent website storage, so websites may keep cookies and login state
on the device. The websites you visit have their own privacy practices. Planner
does not send your browsing history to a developer-operated service.

## Sharing and backups

The Share extension receives only content you choose to share to Planner.
Saving adds that content to your plans; cancelling does not create an item.
When you export a backup, the file contains planning data and is written to
the destination you choose. You control who can access files you share.

## Retention and choices

You can edit and remove saved content in Planner. Synchronization retains
deletion and relationship history to prevent removed data from reappearing.
Removing an item from a list is different from deleting its shared source.

You can control Planner's iCloud access in Apple settings. Removing the app
can remove local data; it does not necessarily remove its private iCloud data.
Use Apple's iCloud storage controls for that data. Keep a backup before removing
an installation that contains plans you need.

## Contact

Proposed support destination: [Planner issues](https://github.com/dvcol/planner/issues).
The owner must approve this public support channel before publication. Do not
post private plans, account details or credentials in a public issue.
