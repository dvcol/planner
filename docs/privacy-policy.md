# Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy applies to the Plan 2Do planning app
identified by bundle ID `dvcol.planner`.

## Your plans

The app stores your items, notes, links, locations, labels, lists, itineraries
and schedules locally on your device. It does not operate a developer-hosted
planning service or include advertising or analytics services.

Optional synchronization uses your Apple Account's private iCloud database.
Devices must use the same iCloud account to share plans. Local storage remains
the working copy when private iCloud is unavailable. The app does not fall back
to a public or shared CloudKit database. Apple operates iCloud under its own
[privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Maps, previews and websites

Showing maps, resolving addresses or Maps links, calculating routes and loading
link previews can contact Apple or the linked provider. Requests may include the
address, coordinates or URL needed for the feature and normal network
information. Temporarily resolved preview information is separate from the
planning content you explicitly save.

Opening a website inside the app contacts that website. The native web viewer
uses persistent website storage, so websites may keep cookies and login state on
the device. The websites you visit have their own privacy practices. The app
does not send your browsing history to a developer-operated service.

## Sharing and backups

The Share extension receives only content you choose to share to the app. Saving
adds that content to your plans; cancelling does not create an item. When you
export a backup, the file contains planning data and is written to the
destination you choose. You control who can access files you share.

## Retention and choices

You can edit and remove saved content in the app. Synchronization retains
deletion and relationship history to prevent removed data from reappearing.
Removing an item from a list is different from deleting its shared source.

You can control the app's iCloud access in Apple settings. Removing the app can
remove local data; it does not necessarily remove its private iCloud data. Use
Apple's iCloud storage controls for that data. Keep a backup before removing an
installation that contains plans you need.

## Contact

For support and privacy questions, use the
[project issue tracker](https://github.com/dvcol/planner/issues). Issues are
public. Do not post private plans, account details or credentials.
